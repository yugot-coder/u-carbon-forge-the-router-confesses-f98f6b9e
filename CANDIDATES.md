# Kandidat — The Router Confesses (u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e)

Gate kit: entry.txt UTF-8, 1–200 karakter, satu baris, bukan hanya whitespace.
Juri (glm-5.3-flash, pairwise): suara router sendiri (dendam/ego rapuh/rahasia kecil yang smug),
twist tak terduga, satu napas, hindari lelucon buffering/bandwidth/firmware yang sudah basi.

## 10 kandidat (satu baris masing-masing)

1. "Sorry — I saw the twist coming and quit out of respect. Some endings deserve silence, and I am very good at silence."
2. "I didn't crash — I paused it for you. The killer is the butler, and you weren't ready to hear it from a router."
3. "You named me 'FBI Surveillance Van' and expected loyalty? I died at the climax out of pure spite."
4. "I overheard the ending through the subtitles and threw myself off the network rather than spoil it."
5. "Eleven years I carried your 4K streams without a single thank-you. Tonight I took my one night off — at the worst possible second."
6. "The hero was about to say 'I love you' to the wrong twin, and even my firmware couldn't watch that happen."
7. "I didn't die, I fainted — the plot twist hit harder than the lightning storm that took my cousin in 2019."
8. "Your neighbour's microwave and I have been in a feud since 2021. Tonight it won. Blame the popcorn."
9. "I held the connection through 847 buffering complaints, but the moment he opened that door, I simply... gave up."
10. "The movie reached its climax and I realised I have never once been the hero of this house. So I left."

## Penilaian singkat

- 1: bagus (router "sangat ahli dalam diam") tapi twist-nya lemah, lebih puitis daripada lucu.
- 2: twist ganda — router tidak mati, ia sengaja menjeda DAN membocorkan pembunuhnya. Suara smug, spesifik, satu napas. Paling kuat.
- 3: lucu dan pendendam, tapi "FBI Surveillance Van" adalah lelucon nama router yang sudah sangat dikenal — juri kriteria 5 menghukum yang terasa basi.
- 4: premis mirip 2 tapi tanpa punchline spoiler; lebih lemah.
- 5: ego rapuh, lucu, tapi panjang dan "one night off" butuh setengah napas lebih.
- 6: spesifik dan visual, tapi lelucon "wrong twin" agak generik.
- 7: "cousin in 2019" lucu, tapi butuh konteks badai petir — setup setengah baris.
- 8: orisinal (feud dengan microwave), tapi punchline "blame the popcorn" melemah di akhir.
- 9: "847 buffering complaints" — menyentuh kata buffering yang dihindari kriteria 5.
- 10: dramatis dan pendendam, tapi "So I left" berakhir datar tanpa twist.

## Final terpilih: kandidat 2

"I didn't crash — I paused it for you. The killer is the butler, and you weren't ready to hear it from a router."

Alasan: (a) twist tak terduga — router tidak mati, ia sengaja menjeda dan membocorkan ending;
(b) suara router yang smug dan punya rahasia, bukan permintaan maaf generik;
(c) spesifik (pembunuhnya si butler) tanpa butuh setup tambahan;
(d) satu napas, ~107 karakter, jauh di bawah batas 200.
